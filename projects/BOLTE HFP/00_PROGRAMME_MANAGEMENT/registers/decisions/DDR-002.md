## Decision ID

DDR-002

## Decision

34-volume documentation structure (Vol 00-33)

## Context

The programme needs one continuous engineering chain rather than disconnected essays (prompt section 36).

## Options Considered

A. 34 volumes per schema, one folder each. B. Fewer, larger volumes. C. Per-discipline wikis.

## Evaluation Criteria

Separation of requirements, design, safety, verification, certification, production and operations; ability to trace across them.

## Evidence

Schema file HFP_Documentation_schema.

## Trade Study

Not performed; structure adopted as supplied.

## Selected Architecture

A, with Volume 33 (MVP) kept separate from production documentation.

## Rationale

Matches the supplied schema and the prompt's documentation hierarchy exactly.

## Consequences

Many small documents; cross-volume traceability must be maintained via the RTM.

## Risks

Orphaned requirements if the RTM is not maintained.

## Revisit Conditions

If a volume proves too small or too large to manage.

_Records are never rewritten. A later contradicting decision creates a new DDR (prompt section 21)._
