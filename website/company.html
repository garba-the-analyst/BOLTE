A Nigerian engineering company with a long view.

BOLTE exists to build the technical capability that lasting independence depends on, beginning with defence and security.

Technological independence begins with the capability to build.

Vision

A technologically independent Nigeria and, in time, a technologically independent Africa.

Mission

To research, engineer, develop and deploy indigenous technologies that strengthen Nigeria's technological foundation.

How we work.

  • Build, do not only buy

    Sovereignty comes from capability. We create technology and the skills to sustain it.

  • Put disciplines together

    Aerospace, radio, software and security progress faster when one team builds them as one system.

  • Honest engineering

    We state limits, risks and maturity plainly, and we publish what we can prove.

  • Maintainable at home

    Everything is designed to be supported by Nigerian engineers, with local parts and local knowledge.

  • Design for Nigerian conditions

    Power cuts, harmattan dust and weak connectivity are design inputs, not exceptions.

  • Grow the community

    We work with universities, researchers and engineers to multiply talent, not just ship products.
