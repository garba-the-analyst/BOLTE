A Nigerian engineering company with a long view.

BOLTE is a research, engineering and technology company focused on indigenous defence and security technology, and on building the capability that technological independence depends on.

Vision

A technologically independent Nigeria and, ultimately, a technologically independent Africa. A country able to research, develop, manufacture and sustain the technologies essential to its security, prosperity and development.

Mission

To research, engineer, develop and deploy indigenous technologies that strengthen Nigeria's technological foundation, with defence and security as our primary focus, and to develop solutions across other strategic sectors.

Aim

To strengthen Nigeria's technological self-reliance through indigenous research, engineering, invention and development, creating the foundation for African technological independence.

Seven goals guide everything we build.

The goals are durable. Our objectives and roadmap change as we learn.

  1. 1

    Indigenous defence and security technology

    Build Nigeria's capacity to research, design, develop and sustain it. This is our primary goal.

  2. 2

    Strengthen the technology backbone

    Engineering capability, research infrastructure, prototyping, manufacturing and software, so Nigeria moves from consuming technology to creating it.

  3. 3

    Turn ideas into inventions

    Find important problems, research, prototype, test and improve until there is a viable product or system.

  4. 4

    Develop Nigerian technological talent

    A community of scientists, engineers, programmers, researchers, designers and inventors, with room to learn, build and commercialise.

  5. 5

    Advance technology across strategic sectors

    Defence, agriculture, energy, healthcare, transportation, robotics, communications, computing and AI, aerospace, environmental and industrial technology.

  6. 6

    Build a research and innovation ecosystem

    Connect universities, researchers, engineers, industry, government and investors, so ideas do not die in labs and notebooks.

  7. 7

    Build technologies for Nigeria, not only import them

    Design around Nigerian environment, resources, infrastructure, economics and security, rather than copying foreign systems.

All problems are solved when you put things together.

Africa's challenges cannot be solved by one discipline, one institution or one person. Progress comes from bringing ideas, people, knowledge, resources and technology together. This is the principle behind how we hire, how we build and who we partner with.

What we value.

  • Sovereignty by capability

    We build, not only buy. Independence comes from being able to design and sustain what a country depends on.

  • Multidisciplinary building

    Aerospace, radio, software and security progress faster when one team builds them as one system.

  • Honest engineering

    We admit limits, risks and maturity, and we publish what we can prove.

  • Local maintainability

    Everything is designed so that Nigerian engineers can support it, with local parts and local knowledge.

  • Talent multiplication

    We train and mentor young engineers and researchers, so every programme grows the people who can build the next one.

Ethics guardrails for our defence work.

These are commitments, and both founders are bound by them.

  • Lawful and legitimate users only

    We comply with Nigerian law and export controls.

  • A defensive posture

    Defence and situational awareness. No technology that enables indiscriminate harm.

  • A human in the loop

    Operational judgements stay with a person. Our alerts are advisory.

  • Safety first

    Testing discipline and documentation come before any deployment.

  • Two signatures for partnerships

    Any partnership with a security institution needs written approval from both co-founders.
