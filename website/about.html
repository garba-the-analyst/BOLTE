About

Identity, vision, mission, aim

BOLTE is a Nigerian research, engineering, and technology company focused primarily on developing indigenous defense and security technologies while building the broader technological capabilities needed to drive Nigeria’s — and ultimately Africa’s — technological independence.

Vision

To contribute to a technologically independent Nigeria and, ultimately, a technologically independent Africa.

A Nigeria that can research, develop, manufacture, and sustain the technologies essential to its security, prosperity, and development.

Mission

To research, engineer, develop, and deploy indigenous technologies that strengthen Nigeria’s technological foundation, with defense and security technology as our primary focus, while developing innovative solutions across other strategic sectors.

Overall aim

To strengthen Nigeria’s technological self-reliance through indigenous research, engineering, invention, and technological development, creating the foundation for broader African technological independence.

Method: ask “What does Nigeria need, and how can we build it?” then “What does Africa need, and how can we build it?”

Strategic goals 1–7

1 — Indigenous Defense & Security

PRIMARY

Surveillance, secure comms, robotics/autonomy, logistics, protective tech, intel-support, cyber-resilience, aerospace.

2 — Technology backbone

Engineering, labs, prototyping, manufacturing, software/compute, expertise. Move from consumption to creation.

3 — Ideas into inventions

Problem → research → prototype → test → improve → viable product. An invention engine, not just research.

4 — Talent

Community of scientists, engineers, programmers, researchers, designers, inventors. Learn, build, commercialize.

5 — Strategic sectors

Defense, agriculture, energy, healthcare, transportation, robotics, comms, computing/AI, aerospace, environment, industry.

6 — Ecosystem

Universities → researchers → engineers → industry → government → investors → BOLTE. Ideas must not die in labs.

7 — FOR Nigeria

Design for Nigerian environment, resources, infrastructure, economics, security, capacity. Not copied imports.

Model, values, ethics

PROBLEM → IDEA → RESEARCH → ENGINEERING → PROTOTYPE → TESTING → IMPROVEMENT → DEPLOYMENT → IMPACT

Values: sovereignty by capability · multidisciplinary building · honest engineering · local maintainability · talent multiplication.

Ethics: lawful users only · defensive posture · human in the loop · safety/testing before deployment · both-founders approval for security partnerships.
