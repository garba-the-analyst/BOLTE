About

Identity, vision, mission, aim

BOLTE is a Nigerian research and engineering company focused primarily on developing indigenous defence and security technologies — and the broader technical capabilities needed for lasting technological independence.

🔥

Vision

A technologically independent Nigeria — and, ultimately, a technologically independent Africa.

🛠

Mission

To research, engineer, develop, and deploy indigenous technologies that strengthen Nigeria's technological foundation.

🗹

Overall aim

To strengthen Nigeria's technological self-reliance through indigenous research, engineering, invention, and development.
