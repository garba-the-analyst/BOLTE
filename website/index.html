A technologically independent Nigeria and, ultimately, a technologically independent Africa.

Our mission

To research, engineer, develop and deploy indigenous technologies that strengthen Nigeria's technological foundation, with defence and security as our primary focus.

Our philosophy

All problems are solved when you put things together. Ideas, people, knowledge, resources and technology make progress only when they are combined.

The BOLTE model: from a Nigerian need to real impact.

Every programme follows the same path. We widen the loop over time, from Nigerian solutions to African capability, and on to African technological independence.

  1. ProblemStart with a Nigerian need
  2. IdeaFrame a solution
  3. ResearchStudy what is known
  4. EngineeringDesign it properly
  5. PrototypeBuild a working version
  6. TestingBreak it on purpose
  7. ImprovementFix it, then retest
  8. DeploymentPut it in real hands
  9. ImpactMeasure what changed
