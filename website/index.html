AeroPulse-NG: airspace surveillance that runs offline.

It turns low-cost software-defined radio hardware and an ordinary PC into an air-traffic and tactical surveillance workstation. No internet connection required.

Receives

Alerts

Commodity SDR hardware and a standard PC
