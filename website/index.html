What we do

Three commitments.

Defense-first R&D

Indigenous surveillance, secure communications, robotics and protective technologies — legitimate, lawful, locally manufacturable and maintainable.

Offline-first engineering

Systems that work through harmattan, outages and terrain shadow. Air-gapped by contract: no feature may require the internet.

Talent & ecosystem

Scientists, engineers, programmers and inventors building together — connecting universities, industry, government and investors so ideas don’t die in labs.
