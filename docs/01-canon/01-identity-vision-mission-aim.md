# Identity, Vision, Mission, Aim

## Identity in one sentence
BOLTE is a Nigerian research, engineering, and technology company focused primarily on developing indigenous defense and security technologies while building the broader technological capabilities needed to drive Nigeria's — and ultimately Africa's — technological independence.

## 1. Vision
To contribute to a technologically independent Nigeria and, ultimately, a technologically independent Africa.

A Nigeria that possesses the capacity to research, develop, manufacture, and sustain the technologies essential to its security, prosperity, and development, rather than relying excessively on foreign technological systems.

## 2. Mission
To research, engineer, develop, and deploy indigenous technologies that strengthen Nigeria's technological foundation, with defense and security technology as our primary focus, while developing innovative solutions across other strategic sectors.

As BOLTE grows, these capabilities will extend beyond Nigeria to contribute to Africa's technological independence.

## 3. Overall Aim
To strengthen Nigeria's technological self-reliance through indigenous research, engineering, invention, and technological development, creating the foundation for broader African technological independence.

## Scope notes
- Primary: indigenous defense & security technology (legitimate, lawful, locally manufacturable/maintainable).
- Secondary: strategic sectors where technology solves an important Nigerian problem (see Goals).
- Method: ask "What does Nigeria need, and how can we build it?" then "What does Africa need, and how can we build it?"
