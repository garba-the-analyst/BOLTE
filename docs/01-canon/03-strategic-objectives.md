# Strategic Objectives (what BOLTE will actually do)

## Research & Development
- Establish dedicated R&D programs; identify problems of strategic importance; research indigenous solutions; develop/test functional prototypes; iterate continuously.

## Defense & Security
- Primary R&D specialization; improve security, situational awareness, communications, logistics, protection, operational resilience; collaborate with appropriate institutions on legitimate research; ensure local manufacture/maintenance/improvement.

## Innovation & Invention
- Idea→prototype pipeline; evaluation mechanisms; protect/develop IP; commercialize viable tech where appropriate.

## Human Capital
- Train/mentor young Nigerian scientists, engineers, programmers, inventors; multidisciplinary teams; practical experimentation + academic research.

## Infrastructure
- Develop labs, workshops, prototyping facilities as BOLTE grows; internal capability to design/test/iterate locally.

## Collaboration
- Partner with universities, research institutions, industry, government, tech orgs; connect inventors to resources.

## Expansion
- Nigeria as initial base; scale nationally; extend across Africa as company matures.
