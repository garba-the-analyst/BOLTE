# Strategic Goals 1–7

## Goal 1 — Indigenous Defense & Security Technology (primary)
Build Nigeria's capacity to research, design, develop, and sustain indigenous defense and security technologies.
Areas: surveillance and reconnaissance; secure communications; robotics and autonomous systems; military logistics; protective technologies; intelligence-support systems; cybersecurity and technological resilience; aerospace and related technologies.

## Goal 2 — Strengthen Nigeria's Technology Backbone
Address foundational weaknesses: engineering capabilities; research infrastructure; prototyping facilities; manufacturing capabilities; software and computing infrastructure; indigenous expertise. Move from consumption to creation.

## Goal 3 — Turn Ideas Into Inventions
Identify important problems → research → prototype → test → improve → viable product/system. BOLTE is a problem-solving and invention engine, not just a research org.

## Goal 4 — Develop Nigerian Technological Talent
Build community of scientists, engineers, programmers, researchers, designers, inventors. Create opportunities to learn, experiment, collaborate, build, commercialize. Extend model across Africa over time.

## Goal 5 — Advance Technology Across Strategic Sectors
Defense & Security; Agriculture; Energy; Healthcare; Transportation; Robotics & Automation; Communications; Computing & AI; Aerospace; Environmental technology; Industrial technology. Rule: if technology can solve an important problem, BOLTE should be capable of exploring it.

## Goal 6 — Build a Nigerian Research & Innovation Ecosystem
Connect Universities → Researchers → Engineers → Industry → Government → Investors → BOLTE. Bridge: Idea → Research → Prototype → Testing → Product → Deployment. Prevent ideas dying in classrooms/labs/papers/heads.

## Goal 7 — Build Technologies FOR Nigeria, Not Simply Import INTO Nigeria
Design around Nigerian realities: environment, resources, infrastructure, economics, security challenges, human capacity. Not mere copying of foreign tech.
