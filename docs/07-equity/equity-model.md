# Equity & Profit Model — v2.0 Two-Layer (Pre-CAC + Incorporation Design)

Source pack: `docs/BOLTE critical/2. Equity & Profit Model (v1.0).docx` (Garba, 02 Oct 2026) — draft for Nigerian counsel + tax adviser review before anything is allotted or paid. This file is the working summary; the DOCX is authoritative where they differ.

Core principle: **cash and ownership are separate questions.** Layer A splits cash while unincorporated. Layer B designs share ownership at incorporation. Neither implies the other.

## Layer A — Cash waterfall (DECIDED 30 Sept 2026, both founders)

Contractual split of net gains (prizes, grants, sponsorship, revenue minus receipted direct costs). Runs from the Effective Date (02 Oct 2026) until the CAC certificate date, then sunsets: earned amounts paid within 60 days, unspent Treasury money becomes Company reserves. After that, cash follows shareholding through dividends.

Example: N = ₦1,000,000 → Treasury ₦700,000; founders ₦60k each; B1/B2/C/D ₦45k each.

Rules: eligible only if NDA + IP + MOU signed, no unresolved misconduct, Contribution Standard met (or approved leave). Pro-rata by Active Weeks from Effective Date (or last distribution). **Reserve first:** no payout until three months of planned costs are held. Both founders decide timing. Prizes follow the same split unless sub-team + both founders agree otherwise in writing before submission. Forfeited/unclaimed → Treasury. Payments are the recipient's taxable income — accountant sets withholding/reporting before first payout.

Fairness note (from the pack): Layer A pays members more in total (18) than founders (12) — workable only because it is short-lived and carries no ownership. Revisit before the first payout if large sums are expected pre-incorporation.

Founders' split options (select ONE before CAC filing): **A** 35/30 with 5-pt IP premium (table default) · **B** 32.5/32.5 equal · **C** scorecard outcome. Scorecard criteria (each founder scores both, 0–10, discuss gaps ≥3): weekly time 25% · product/IP delivered 20% · role risk 15% · cash/assets 15% · expertise/network 15% · opportunity cost 10%. If within 10% of equal, split equally and handle IP via the Deed.

Vesting: Contribution Standard per seat (MOU Schedule B); missed months pause (founders may restore in writing); reverse vesting (allot now, Company repurchase right lapses monthly) vs options — counsel + tax decide. Leavers: Good (keeps vested, unvested repurchased nominal) · Neutral (keeps vested, unvested lapse, 12-mo Fair-Value call) · Bad/Cause (unvested lapse, vested callable at lower of nominal/Fair Value — counsel to confirm). No acceleration on investment; 50% single-trigger on sale + termination without Cause within 12 mo (counsel to confirm).

Dilution at pre-seed (reserve unchanged): 10% round → Garba 31.5 / Frances 27.0 / each Core 4.05 / Adviser 0.9 / Reserve 14.4 · 15% → 29.75 / 25.5 / 3.825 / 0.85 / 13.6 · 20% → 28.0 / 24.0 / 3.6 / 0.8 / 12.8. Investors often demand pool top-up pre-money (dilutes holders) — plan for it; foreign money is a reserved matter.

After incorporation: dividends pro rata when declared (both founders); Company retains ≥70% of annual distributable profit as reserves until funding or both founders agree (treasury-first in the articles); no founder pay until funding, then board-set stipend with Adviser input.

Mechanics at CAC (counsel): statutory minimum issued capital (₦100,000; far higher practical minimum with foreign participation); founders subscribe post Founders' Agreement + IP Deed; board allotments with reverse-vesting agreements; stamp duty; register + PSC filings; articles carry transfer restrictions, pre-emption, drag/tag, leaver calls, reserved matters, deadlock, vesting, confidentiality.

Cap-table hygiene: one register kept by the COO, changed only by board resolution, every grant signed; no side deals (unsigned promises have no effect); annual vesting review; Day-1 changes need both founders + Decision Log entry. See `cap-table-layerB.csv`.
