## 3. Eligibility to receive
Active + NDA + IP assignment signed + no unresolved misconduct + met minimum contribution or approved leave. Joiners/leavers pro-rata by weeks active in the earning period. Unclaimed/forfeited amounts return to treasury.

## 4. Prize-money rule
NDAIE prizes follow the same split unless the sub-team + both founders agree otherwise in writing before submission.

## 5. Transparency
Treasurer publishes ledger monthly; any member may audit receipts. Disputes → founders → mediation.

## 6. Conversion to equity (on CAC Ltd)
Profit %s are a negotiation baseline, not automatic shares. Treasury converts to company funds/unissued shares. Requires shareholders agreement, share allotment, CAC filings, lawyer review.
