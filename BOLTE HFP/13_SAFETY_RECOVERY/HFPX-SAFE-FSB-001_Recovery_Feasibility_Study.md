## 1. Purpose

Attack ISS-008 with first-principles parametrics: quantify what a recovery system must achieve (descent rates, timelines, altitudes) and thereby define the data needed — without claiming feasibility. This study produces data needs, not answers.

## 8. Detailed Design

Parametric results (script output, NOT claims): at mid-weight with Cd 1.5 (REF-CLASS TBC), Vt ranges ≈8.3 m/s (20 m²) down to ≈4.2 m/s (80 m²); weight band 1079–1471 N widens each figure by ≈±8%. Minimum-altitude table crosses timeline placeholders against deceleration placeholders to show the trade surface \$h_{min} = V_t·t + V_t²/2a\$. Limiting-case finding: in hover (≈0 m AGL) no parachute-type timeline closes — the hover case must be carried by automatic stabilisation (13.10), impact protection (13.12/13.13), or escape (13.14), all effectiveness-TBD. ISS-008 therefore stays OPEN with quantified data needs: canopy aerodynamics, timeline allocations, human tolerance criteria, deployment reliability, test articles.
